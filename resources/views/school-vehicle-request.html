School Vehicle Request

School Vehicle Request is an internal system for teachers and staff of Chalidana Islamic School (CIS Menganti) to request school vehicles.

What the app does

Teachers and staff sign in with their Google account and submit the date, purpose, and destination of their trip. All requests are recorded in a Google Sheet that only authorized school administrators can access.

How the app uses your Google account

The app uses your Google sign-in only to identify the requester (name and email address). It does not use this information for any other purpose and does not share it with third parties.
